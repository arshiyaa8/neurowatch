📄 Peer-Reviewed Academic Research Paper

"NeuroWatch AI: A Rule-Based, Explainable Bystander Screening System for Early Stroke Sign Recognition in Low-Resource and Rural Settings"

Author: Muskan Bharti (Jaypee Institute of Information Technology)

DOI & Publication: Zenodo Record 10.5281/zenodo.22854369

Core Research Insights:
  • Bystander Bottleneck: In rural and low-resource settings across India, early facial drooping and arm weakness are frequently misread, missing the golden 4.5-hour thrombolysis window.
  • Explainable AI: Uses smartphone cameras to measure facial asymmetry and arm drift deterministically without black-box classifiers, prioritizing medical explainability.
  • Screening Aid: Serves as an immediate bystander decision aid to accelerate Indian National Emergency (108) ambulance dispatch.

🧠 Understanding BE-FAST Emergency Signs

B — Balance: Sudden loss of balance or difficulty walking.
E — Eyes: Sudden double vision or loss of sight in one or both eyes.
F — Face Drooping: One side of the face droops or is numb when smiling.
A — Arm Drift: One arm drifts downward when raising both arms.
S — Speech Slurring: Speech is slurred or difficult to understand.
T — Time to Call 108: "Time is brain". Immediately tap the Green Emergency SOS button.
